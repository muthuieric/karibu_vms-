# Karibu VMS Overview

Karibu VMS is a visitor management system for facilities that need a clearer way to record arrivals, manage entry points, support guards, and review visitor history.

It supports QR self check-in, guard desk registration, visitor intake rules, host and department setup, checkout tracking, restricted visitor records, billing summaries, and M-Pesa payment initiation.
